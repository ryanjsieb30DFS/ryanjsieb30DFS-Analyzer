## Build it like a sharp

1. **The anchor decision.** The fork is Scheffler ($13,400, 23.7% and rising) — with him, the build goes stars-and-scrubs; without him, the sharp anchors the $10K-$11K tier. The anchor-equivalence pair there is Burns (36.5%) vs Hideki (20.9%), $300 apart — a sharp who wants that tier leans toward the lower-owned course-history half, or Fleetwood at 11.8%.
2. **The leverage piece.** A sharp's one dart lives where the squeeze starves ownership: Schauffele 7.4%, Fitzpatrick 7.3%, Thomas 3.2%, Koivun 5.6%. On this tough-side-of-the-cusp day the format history says one dart, at most two.
3. **What a sharp refuses.** Carrying Im + Burns means sharing that slice with ~35 of the 196 entries before a shot is hit. A sharp also won't stack two of the steamed cheap names (Harman, Poston, Noren) in one lineup.
4. **The salary shape.** Scheffler takes $13,400, leaving $36,600 — an average of $7,320 across five slots, which is exactly why the field's Scheffler builds all buy the same $6K-$7K chalk. The real decision in a Scheffler build is WHICH steamed cheap name to skip; the article's answer is max one golfer below $7,000. A no-Scheffler build averages $8,333 a slot and lives in the ignored 8Ks.
5. **Two contests, two entries.** One lineup, one contest. The 141-field Dogleg pays top-heavy — that entry wants the higher-variance read (the un-owned second dart) because only the top matters. The 196-field flat Albatross pays many similar prizes — that entry wants the clean envelope build (~19-23% average ownership, one dart) because solid-and-different cashes fine.
