## Write-ups

1. **Puka Nacua** — $11,200, 65.5% owned / 23.8% captain (ETR), proj 22.2, ceiling 37.0. He led the whole league with a 38% target share when Adams was on the field, meaning nearly 4 of every 10 Rams throws went to him (Matchups). Against the 49ers last year he caught 10 for 85 and a score, then 5 for 64 and a score. His best realistic night (his ceiling) is the highest on the slate, and the Breakdown calls him the best play at both spots. The catch is the crowd: about 2 of every 3 teams have him, and 1 in 4 make him captain. — Core

2. **Christian McCaffrey** — $10,600, 65.0% owned / 20.5% captain (ETR), proj 21.3, ceiling 35.5. He handled 70% of the 49ers' carries and 81% of the carries inside the 5-yard line last year, plus 1 of every 5 targets (Breakdown). The Breakdown says he is "severely underpriced" here, about $1,255 under his usual Showdown price. The risk is a rushing offense that fell from 5th to 23rd in efficiency while Kittle was out (Matchups). He and Nacua are owned almost identically, so the crowd treats them as the same pick (anchor twins). — Core

3. **Davante Adams** — $9,200, 34.8% owned / 8.4% captain (ETR), proj 14.9, ceiling 28.0. He had 27 end-zone targets last year, 9 more than any other receiver in football, and 34 red-zone targets to Nacua's 21 (Matchups, Breakdown). That makes him the Rams' touchdown man, and about 1 in 12 teams captain him against 1 in 4 for Nacua. The Breakdown warns that when Adams tops 20 points, Nacua averaged only 20.1, so the two fight over the same big nights. — Good

4. **Mike Evans** — $8,200, 27.7% owned / 4.9% captain (ETR), proj 12.4, ceiling 24.0. He is the Breakdown's one named 49ers captain pick, "the best price-adjusted 49ers pass catcher" with a 21.5% target share and 7 end-zone targets in 2025. He was still targeted on 27% of his routes against zone coverage at age 32, and the Rams played zone on 80% of snaps (Matchups). Only about 1 in 20 teams captain him, so his big game beats the crowd where it is thinnest (leverage). The risk is a brand-new offense with five catchers sharing the ball. — Good · Leverage

5. **Brock Purdy** — $9,400, 52.5% owned / 6.4% captain (ETR), proj 18.8, ceiling 29.0. He finished as a top-12 quarterback three straight years, and the 49ers scored 26 points in both Rams games last year even with a backup (Matchups). The Breakdown says he can be "the best price-adjusted option" if the targets spread evenly across his new catchers. His floor is real but his ceiling needs the 49ers to trail and throw, which happens only 4 of 10 times when they trail (the Breakdown's 64% drop-back rate). — Good

6. **Kyren Williams** — $8,600, 37.2% owned / 8.2% captain (ETR), proj 14.7, ceiling 26.2. The Breakdown lists him as a captain leverage play because the crowd chases Nacua, McCaffrey and Adams first. The risk is the reported "pretty close to 50-50" carry split with Corum. — Good
