# NFL Showdown — Broncos at Chiefs (Monday night, Week 1) — slate strategy

_DraftKings only. Every FanDuel number in the articles was dropped._

## The short version

This section is the whole slate in nine plain sentences.

1. This is a one-game contest where the betting market expects a close, lowish-scoring game between two good defenses.
2. The points come from a handful of stars, and the two writers agree the Chiefs' new running back Kenneth Walker is the safest path to a big score.
3. The crowd is stacking both quarterbacks (Mahomes and Nix) together and putting Walker in the Captain spot, the spot that scores 1.5 times the points.
4. The one decision that separates entries this week is who wears the Captain tag, because the crowd's captains are all the same four names.
5. A sharp puts the Captain tag on a pass-catcher the crowd is skipping for that spot, like Travis Kelce or Courtland Sutton, and keeps the quarterbacks as normal players.
6. A sharp also leaves a little salary unspent, because the exact-cap lineup of four stars plus the cheapest fillers is the one the crowd copies thousands of times.
7. The biggest trap is Patrick Mahomes at a 7-in-10 pick rate, because the matchup writer calls him a below-average quarterback play in his first game back from knee surgery.
8. The second trap is paying the crowd's price for the Chiefs' kicker when the Broncos' kicker projects the same and is picked less.
9. In a field of 47,562 with five entries, each entry should tell a different story of how the game goes.

## Edges & tensions

This section lists the things that actually matter on this slate. It states them and stops; the calls are yours.

1. **Mahomes is a trap-shaped price** · ETR sheet #1 at 17.7 points and 69.7% owned, but Silva's Matchups calls him a QB2: 11.8-point average in his last three games against this defense, no starting left tackle, first game back from a torn ACL.
2. **Anchor-Equivalence (two players the crowd treats as the same bet)** · Mahomes and Nix are twins: $9,600 vs $9,800, 17.7 vs 17.4 projected, 13.6% vs 13.1% captain ownership. Rice and Waddle are a second twin pair: 14.7 vs 13.6 projected, 12.5% vs 9.5% captain.
3. **The captain gap** · the crowd's captains are Walker 16.3%, Mahomes 13.6%, Nix 13.1%, Rice 12.5%; the pass-catcher captains under 6% are Kelce 5.4% (Silva: 9 catches for 124, then 6/58, 8/64/1, 9/91/1 in his last four vs Denver), Sutton 5.1%, Dobbins 5.7%.
4. **The game scripts** · a 43.5 total sits in the middle band where RB, WR and QB captains win about equally; a 2.5-point spread makes a blowout unlikely; Silva's 17–14 prediction is a slog where the running back captain has won 46% of the time.
5. **Duplication (copies of the same lineup)** · the field pairs Mahomes + Nix in ~44.8% of lineups, about 21,300 entries; all four stars (Walker, Mahomes, Nix, Rice) sit together in ~12%, about 5,800 entries, and that core plus Bryant plus a sub-$2,000 filler lands within $500 of the cap.
6. **Writer vs vendor on Sutton** · the Showdown Breakdown calls him leverage "if ownership remains low", but ETR's own sheet has him 28.3% owned at flex; only his 5.1% captain slot is actually low. Silva adds that Pat Bryant out-targeted him 26% to 19% last year.
7. **Kicker mispricing** · Butker 26.5% owned at 8.5 projected vs Lutz 22.1% at 8.2 — same points, Lutz is the underdog kicker in a close outdoor game. The bundle flags Butker and the Broncos defense as this slate's other trap-shaped prices (Chiefs D is $400 cheaper and projects 6.3 vs 6.0).
8. **The cheap slot leans Denver** · the crowd's fillers are Bryant 22.9% and Engram 17.5%; the Chiefs' cheap catchers Thornton 11.6% and Gray 7.5% are the quiet side. Last slate's leverage capture was 0%, so the sub-$2,600 role-path screen (Emmett Johnson, Coleman) is this slate's priority.
9. **Walker's ceiling is conditional** · 55.9% owned with the sheet's highest ceiling (29.8), but Denver allowed the 3rd-lowest explosive-run rate (6.1%) and Silva says Walker only hits his ceiling if he runs more routes than rookie Emmett Johnson.
10. **Team split** · ETR's build projections say the field goes 3-3 36.8%, 4 Chiefs–2 Broncos 26.7%, 2 Chiefs–4 Broncos 23.8%, 5 Chiefs–1 Bronco 9.2%, and 1 Chief–5 Broncos only 3.5% — the Broncos onslaught is Silva's predicted result and the least-built shape.

## Field vs Sharp — how this slate gets played

This section compares what most entries will look like with what a sharp entry looks like.

**How the FIELD will play it.** The typical entry captains Walker or one of the quarterbacks, then carries both Mahomes and Nix at flex, Rice, and a cheap Denver filler (Bryant or Engram) to fit under the cap. ETR's build projection says 3-3 is the most common split at 36.8%, and the chalk pairs above mean the average entry shares its core with thousands of others.

**How a SHARP will play it.** A sharp still anchors on the same star tier (winners here average about 45% ownership per spot, so fading stars is not the edge), but wears the Captain tag on a pass-catcher the crowd is not captaining: Kelce, Sutton, Waddle, or Dobbins. The sharp carries ONE quarterback most of the time instead of both, leaves $500–$1,500 unspent, and puts one sub-5% piece in two or three of five entries (youdacao, the pro who beat you 2 of 6 times, carries a sub-5% piece in most lineups at ~17.6% ownership per spot).

**The gap.** The field pays 13.6% captain ownership for Mahomes and 16.3% for Walker; the sharp gets a similar 26–30 point captain ceiling from Kelce (30.3 captain ceiling) or Waddle (38 captain ceiling) at 5.4% and 9.5% captain ownership. The field spends $19,400 on two quarterbacks who project 17 points each; the sharp spends one of those salaries on a captain ceiling instead.

## Build it like a sharp

This section walks through the decisions a sharp makes, in order, for THIS slate. Suggestions are in sharp-voice; the picks are yours. Every named shape comes from the framework's thesis vocabulary.

1. **The anchor decision.** A sharp anchors from the star tier (all four stars sit at 41–70% ownership and winners here run about 45% per spot), but chooses the Captain from the under-captained ceiling paths: Waddle 9.5%, Kelce 5.4%, Sutton 5.1%, Dobbins 5.7%, with Walker 16.3% reserved for the Chiefs-control entry. The twins Mahomes/Nix and Rice/Waddle are one bet each; the sharp holds ONE quarterback at flex in most entries, and it is Nix more often than the crowd's Mahomes.

2. **The leverage piece.** The sub-5% piece lives in the cheap slot, at a rate of about two or three of five entries, never every entry: Emmett Johnson 4.9%, Jonah Coleman 4.4%, Thornton 11.6% (the half-price Chiefs answer to Bryant), Mims 5.6%. youdacao's pattern (a sub-5% piece in most lineups) is the coaching target; needlunchmoney's zero-leverage, 45%-per-spot card is the one that has not beaten you.

3. **What the field duplicates.** Mahomes + Nix together is about 21,300 entries, and the four-star core is about 5,800. A pair count is information, not a rule. On this slate the sharp refuses the Mahomes + Nix pair AT FLEX in most entries for a stated reason: a 43.5 total and Silva's QB2 read on Mahomes make two 17-point quarterbacks for $19,400 a low-ceiling use of 39% of the cap. Rice + Waddle both at flex is fine in the competitive-middle entry; holding both AND both quarterbacks is the copied shape.

4. **The salary shape.** Captain Walker costs $15,900 and leaves $34,100 for five spots, about $6,800 each. Add Nix ($9,800) and Waddle ($9,000) and the last three spots average $5,100, so the real decision is which star to drop: Rice ($9,400) or Kelce ($7,000). The median winner left $1,400 unspent and only 7% spent the full $50,000, so a sharp keeps every entry at or under $49,500.

5. **Five entries, five stories (one contest declared, so the split is by script, not contest).** Competitive middle: a pass-catcher captain (Waddle or Rice) with one quarterback and a bring-back from the other side — 89% of passing-script winners had one. Slog / ground-and-pound: Dobbins or Walker captain with his own defense and the matching kicker (Lutz with the Broncos, Butker with the Chiefs), 4-2 or 5-1. Chiefs blowout 5-1: Walker captain, Mahomes, two Chiefs catchers, one Broncos bring-back catcher, never the Broncos defense. Broncos upset 5-1 (the 3.5%-built shape and Silva's predicted result): Sutton or Dobbins captain, Nix, the Broncos defense, one Chiefs bring-back. Garbage-time 2-4 tilted away from the captain: Walker or Kelce captain with four Broncos catching up late (Harvey, Waddle, Sutton, Bryant).

6. **The big-field attack** (a 150-Max in a 47,562 field). One line per field mistake with slate evidence:
   - Self-copying · Mahomes + Nix in ~44.8% of entries · one quarterback per entry.
   - Mispriced ownership · Butker 26.5% vs Lutz 22.1% at the same projection · the underdog kicker in the slog entry.
   - Recency / name chasing · Mahomes 69.7% on reputation vs Silva's 11.8-point matchup average · the Nix twin.
   - Anchor-twin herding · Mahomes/Nix and Rice/Waddle captained at 13.6/13.1% and 12.5/9.5% · the less-captained twin.
   - Ignored mid-price captains · Kelce 5.4%, Sutton 5.1%, Dobbins 5.7% at captain vs 28–33% at flex · captain from this tier.
   - News stampede · Bryant "biggest winner of camp" → 22.9% at $3,200 · Thornton or a role-path dart in that slot.
   - Dead builds · the four stars + Bryant + a sub-$2,000 filler at $49,500+ · salary left and one dart.
   - Trap-shaped prices · Mahomes, Butker, Broncos DST owned ahead of projection · the cheaper twin in each spot.
   The effective field is far smaller than 47,562: the entries that avoid these mistakes compete against the pros' portfolios, not the crowd's copies. Still zero lineups here.

7. **The pre-lock check.** Ask each entry:
   - Does it name its script (competitive middle, slog, Chiefs blowout, Broncos upset, garbage-time) and does the team split match it?
   - If Mahomes and Nix are both in it at flex, what is the reason beyond "both project 17"?
   - Is it at exactly $50,000, or the four stars + Bryant + min-price shape?
   - If a kicker is in it, is he next to his own defense rather than his own Captain quarterback, and are kicker + defense pieces at two or fewer?
   - Is the Captain a ceiling bet (a catcher, Walker, or Dobbins in the slog) and not the cheapest tag on the sheet?

**Envelope target:** about 35–45% ownership per spot across the five entries, one sub-5% piece in two or three of them, $500+ unspent, no exact copies — youdacao's shape (sub-5% piece in most lineups, ~17.6% per spot in small fields) adjusted up for Showdown's chalkier winners.
