The risk is real: Brett picks Rodrigues to knock him out, and he only defends takedowns at 68%. — Core
2. **Lerryan Douglas** — $9,300, 38% (DailyFan), proj 83.9, ceiling 103.5. He wins with one clean power shot early. He scored 122 in his debut and 138 on the Contender Series, and he is -250 to win before the final bell (the Matchups article). The Top Plays article makes him Brett's number-one target in the expensive tier. He has been knocked out twice and submitted twice, so a loss is a zero. — Core

If Hernandez survives and starts wrestling, Rodrigues likely drowns. — Good
