**The Anchor-Equivalence call was right, and it is the reason two of your teams survived.**

Anchor-Equivalence means: when two expensive players cost about the same and the crowd picks them about equally, the crowd treats them as the same bet — so taking the less-popular one gets you the same upside with fewer people sharing it.

The strategy surfaced two such pairs.

- **Oliveira and Rebecki.** Both hit — 116.85 and 143.72. Rebecki was the top score on the whole slate. Either was fine.
- **Medić and Cepo.** These two split by **127 points.** Medić scored 129.52. Cepo scored 2.8.

Every good finish you had was a Medić team. Every bad finish was a Cepo team. The rule worked exactly as written.

**But there is a sharper version of the rule you already own and did not use.** Your framework has a rule saying: when one side of an equivalent pair is BOTH the most-picked player AND a "knockout or nothing" fighter with no backup way to score, put more weight on the OTHER side — do not split 50/50. Cepo met both conditions, and your own strategy document wrote them down: *"13 of 14 wins in round one," "legit bad after two minutes: no cardio, no ground game, 0-2 in fights that reach round three."* Then it framed the pair as a simple carry-one-or-the-other choice, and your portfolio split evenly — Medić in 2 of 4, Cepo in 2 of 4.

**What to do about it next slate:** when you write down that a popular player has no backup plan if the knockout does not come, that sentence is the instruction. Weight away from him instead of flipping a coin.

### 4. ADOPT — a distance fight is not a low ceiling

**From:** `mma-se-2026-07-12-distance-fight-is-not-low-ceiling` (3 confirming slates: 7/12, 7/19, 8/2)

**Add to `framework.md`, new section — "Distance ≠ Low Ceiling (8/2/26 — 3-slate validated)":**

A distance projection caps the ceiling ONLY when it is also low-volume and low-control — a true point-fighting stalemate.

> **What does NOT count as structural (added 8/2/26, after two failures).**
>
> A fade justified by **finish probability alone** — "inside-the-distance odds are long," "this fight profiles slow," "he needs a knockout to pay off" — is a narrative fade wearing structural clothing.
