## Write-ups

1. **Christopher Bell** — $10,200, 46% (DailyFan), proj 77.0. Dustin's Deep Dive calls him "my favorite play on the slate by a mile": he was #1 in every practice lap average, took 52.7% of all fast laps at Phoenix this year, and won the All-Star race at this track last year. In a 450-lap race where the front row supplied the main dominator in 8 of the 10 most recent flat-track optimals (DFR data), he starts P3 with the slate's highest projection. The risk is simply sharing him with nearly half the field — and note your own SE fields have crowded Bell in 2 of 2 past contests (field crowds). — Core
2. **Ryan Blaney** — $10,700, 47% (DailyFan), proj 68.0. He is handed the pole at a track where the polesitter led 292, 304, and 208 laps in three of the recent comparable races (DFR laps-led data), and DDD says "Blaney on short flat tracks is the man" — a top-3 car on this track type for five straight years. The pole here is the single most valuable piece of real estate on the slate because passing the leader is brutally hard on the repave. The risk is DDD's own caveat: he could lose the lead on lap 1 to Gibbs and never lead at all, leaving a good-not-great score at 47% ownership. — Core
3. **Alex Bowman** — $7,000, 28% (DailyFan), proj 41.4. DDD's CORE value call: "Bowman at 7k is a steal in this spot" — top-10 practice lap averages this weekend, 4th in last year's All-Star race here, and starting P25 gives him real room to gain positions (place-differential points). A solid top-15 gets him ~40 points and a top 10 clears 50, huge at this price. The risk is ordinary: he plateaus around 15th and posts a fine-not-slate-winning score. — Core
4. **Joey Logano** — $9,500, 24% (DailyFan), proj 54.2. DDD's GPP Fav: he led 199 of 200 laps to win the 2024 All-Star race here, ran top-8 lap times in practice, and the 450-lap distance means front-runners rack up fast-lap points that raise their whole scoring range. His ceiling is a race win with 100+ laps led at half the ownership of the two anchors ahead of him. The risk is that Blaney and Bell simply control the race from the front row and Logano settles for a quiet top 5. — Good
5. **Josh Berry** — $7,100, 24% (DailyFan), proj 42.4. DDD's GPP FAV in the value range: nearly all of his ~8 top-10 finishes the last two years came on exactly this style of short track, and starting P28 gives him huge position-gain equity — a P13 finish alone is 45 points. The Penske short-track program is the engine behind the speed. The risk is his own record: he wrecks a lot, and the many 25th-or-worse finishes are real. — Good
6. **Ty Gibbs** — $8,500, 22% (DailyFan), proj 44.8. He carries the same front-row dominator access as the two chalk anchors at less than half their ownership. The risk is the flip side of that same start: Blaney and Bell are right there, and if he never leads he can finish 8th with a bad score. — Good

The knock is structural: 38% ownership is dominator-level chalk for a driver whose path is passing cars at a track where the DFR data says gains from deep in the field are largely unrealized. — Good
