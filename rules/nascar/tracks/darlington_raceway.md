# Darlington Raceway

**UNVERIFIED — built from this slate's articles only (9/6/26 Southern 500 slate: Dustin's Deep Dive + DFR historical charts).**

## Track profile
- 1.33-mile egg-shaped oval ("The Lady in Black"). Turns 1-2 have a wider radius than turns 3-4; drivers run the high groove against the wall. Small mistakes earn the "Darlington stripe" or a DNF (DDD 9/6/26).
- Abrasive, high tire-wear surface — tire management defines long runs; fast laps spread out across the field more than laps led do (DDD).
- New (2026) higher-horsepower / lower-downforce package ran here in the spring: only one DNF; rewarded elite tire managers rather than adding chaos (DDD).
- Southern 500 = 367 laps, the long points-heavy crown-jewel race.

## Dominator structure
- Mega-dominator track: in 5 of the 6 races before 9/6/26, one driver led between 174 and 309 laps (DFR laps-led chart).
- Laps led concentrate in the front rows: nearly all laps led come from the top-6 starting spots; fast laps are spread through the top 10 (DFR chart).
- In 13 of the 15 races before 9/6/26, the optimal lineup had two top-10 starters with at least one starting 6th or better (DDD).
- 0 to 3 dominators have appeared in Next Gen optimals; a driver starting 5th or better was optimal in each of the 9 races before this slate (DDD).

## Optimal-lineup start-position mix (last 9 races, DFR optimal-lineup table)
- Top 5 starts: 27.8% of optimal slots · 6th-10th: ~5.5% · 11th-20th: 24.1% · **21st-30th: 33.3% (the biggest band)** · worse than 30th: **9.3%** (~0.6 of 6 slots per race).
- Deep-back (P30+) hits happen (SVG P36 spring 2025, Berry P33 + Gragson P36 spring 2024) but are the exception, not the base.
