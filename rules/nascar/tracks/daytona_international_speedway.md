# Daytona International Speedway

**UNVERIFIED — built from this slate's articles only (2026-08-29 Coke Zero Sugar 400 night race: Dustin's Deep Dive + the DFR starting-position table + the DFR nine-race optimal-lineup chart). No results data logged here yet. Confirm every number at the first Daytona autopsy.**

## Track profile

- **2.5-mile asphalt oval — a superspeedway.** Drafting is the whole game: running in the pack is the fastest way around, and it is also why the carnage happens.
- **Double-digit DNFs in each of the last 12 races here.** Place differential is the dominant scoring source.
- **The summer night race has historically been more chaotic than the February Daytona 500** (Dustin's Deep Dive, 8/29/26).
- Same pack-racing family as reconfigured Atlanta — see `atlanta_motor_speedway.md`, whose 2026-07-12 running is the only results-verified pack-track data in this repo.
- **2026 note:** NASCAR made rule changes to the cars for this specific event. Effect unknown — could mean easier passing (more wrecks) or better handling (fewer DNFs). Treat all historical rates below as pre-change base rates.
- **New stage lengths for this race** mean the underfunded open cars may not need a green-flag stop until the final stage, which can keep them on the lead lap 3-5 spots longer than usual (Dustin's Deep Dive).

## Dominators / laps led

- **Dominator points are nearly irrelevant.** Only **1.5 drivers per race** who led double-digit laps also posted a top-10 DK score. A driver can lead 40 laps for 10 dominator points and then lose 5 spots, wiping the gain out.
- The framework's standing "two dominator paths minimum" rule is explicitly overridden here by track data.
- If picking a leader: the front-row-adjacent Team Penske cars (Cindric, Logano) have led the most laps at drafting tracks (Dustin's Deep Dive, 8/29/26).

## Salary

- **None of the past 12 Daytona optimal lineups used the full $50,000.** Winning optimal salaries ranged **$31,200 to $49,400**. Leaving salary on the table is normal here, not a mistake.
