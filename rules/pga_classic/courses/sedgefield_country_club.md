# Sedgefield Country Club — Wyndham Championship (Greensboro, NC)

**UNVERIFIED — built from this slate's articles only (2026-08-05, ETR Course Preview + ETG panel). Verify against future slates.**

## Course facts (per ETR Course Preview 8.6.26)
- Donald Ross design (1926, Kris Spence restoration 2007). Par 70, ~7,130 yards — one of the shortest courses on Tour.
- Bermudagrass greens, fairways, and rough (laid 2012). 52 bunkers, water on six holes, pine straw + trees lining most holes.
- Birdie-fest: nine of the last 10 winning scores between -18 and -22. Snedeker shot 59 here (2018). Only two par 5s, both easy; par 3s play under par; ~73% green-hit rate (8th highest of tracked courses).
- The defense is OFF THE TEE: fairways under 30 yards wide (Tour avg ~34), 10th on Tour in out-of-bounds, 5th in cost of missing a fairway into non-rough (pine straw / trees / OB). Missed-fairway penalty above average.
- Approach play grades 2nd easiest on Tour from 150+ yards, 4th easiest inside it. Eight par 4s at 400-450 yards funnel approaches into the 100-175 yard window.
- The course takes back on/around the greens: scrambling bottom-fifth of Tour; lag putting from 15+ feet 10th hardest; large, heavily undulating surfaces.
- Winner archetype (normal year): precise short driver + elite 100-175 wedges/short irons + Bermuda putting. Same course family as Harbour Town, Waialae, River Highlands (Simpson/Kisner/Henley types).
- Soft/wet conditions (2026: soaked Tue/Wed, storms tapering through Sunday) blunt the accuracy edge — fairways hold, greens receptive, lag putting slower/easier. Field bunches; scoring gets even easier.
- ETR course-fit adjustments here are larger than normal, favoring highly accurate, short drivers — even in soft years.
