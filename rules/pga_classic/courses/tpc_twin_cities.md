## Course facts
- Arnold Palmer design in Blaine, MN. Par 71, listed 7,220–7,431 yards depending on setup. Bentgrass greens; flat, open property.
- **Birdie fest with a trapdoor:** water in play on 15 of 18 holes plus scattered OOB. Penalties, reload percentage, and missed-fairway penalty all rank top-15 on Tour. Winning scores -23 (2025, Kitayama) and -24 (2023, Hodges). Expect a Sunday shootout, not a defended lead.
- **Barbell approach distances:** lots of wedges plus long irons (reachable par 5s, long par 4s, par 3s all 175+); the 125–175 middle of the bag is de-emphasized.

## Fit / variance profile (the defining trait)
- Widest "predictive vs explanatory" gap on Tour (ETR): ~40% of scoring variance comes via approach (Tour avg 35%) and ~20% via driving (17%), yet neither approach skill nor driving skill has been PREDICTIVE here — penalty strokes make it far easier to lose strokes than gain them, so skill compresses.
- ETR's course-fit ratings here therefore BOOST short/accurate profiles (2026: Brian Campbell, Putnam, Bezuidenhout, Chatfield, Kuchar, Snedeker, Blair) and DOCK the longest hitters / best ball-strikers (2026: Keith Mitchell, Woodland, Knapp, Brennan, Svensson, Potgieter, Hojgaard, even Scheffler).
