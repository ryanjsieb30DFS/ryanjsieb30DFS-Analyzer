# Detroit Golf Club (Rocket Classic)

**UNVERIFIED — built from this slate's articles only** (2026-07-29 uploads: ETR Course Preview 7.30.26, ETR Large Field Breakdown 7.29.26, ETR Value Report 7.30.26, Establish The Green Rocket Classic panel).

## Course profile (post-renovation, first tournament on the new layout)

- **2026 rebuild:** Tyler Rae's $16.1M restoration of Donald Ross's 1916 North Course design broke ground the day after the 2025 event. This week is the new course's FIRST tournament — every prior stat describes a course that no longer exists.
- **Par 70, 7,300 yards** (was par 72): the two short par 5s became long par 4s, so scores drop ~2 strokes on par change alone. Rae estimates it plays 2–3 strokes harder overall.
- **Bunkers 87 → 103**, deeper, more in driving landing zones. Fairways tightened to ~26 yards at the 300-yard mark (was ~30) — now among the narrower driving tests on Tour.
- **Greens rebuilt ~30% larger** (5,150 → 6,703 sq ft avg), bentgrass, ~11 on the Stimp (slightly slow for Tour). Easier to hit, harder to putt — lots of 30–40 foot lag putts; 10–20 ft mid-range putting matters less.
- **Rough:** 4" fescue/bluegrass mix (was straight bluegrass) — fescue grabs the club. **No water anywhere** (14th-green pond filled in); historic cross-ditches restored.

## The old course (reference only — treat as near-worthless data)

- One of the Tour's easiest venues: winning scores -18 to -26 across seven editions; field hit driver on 86% of tee shots (Tour avg ~70%); leaderboard was "a giant putting contest."
- Winner archetypes were all over the map: Kisner and Merritt alongside DeChambeau, Finau, Potgieter — no single skill demanded. Distance helped; a hot putter decided it.
- Past winners: 2025 Potgieter (-22, playoff over Greyserman/Kirk), 2024 Cam Davis (-18), 2023 Fowler (-24), 2022 Finau (-26), 2021 Cam Davis (-18).

- **2026-08-03 (post-slate, Rocket Classic 7/30 — FIRST PLAY OF THE NEW LAYOUT; this section is now partly verified):**
  - **The rebuild did NOT suppress scoring the way ETR's 2–3-strokes-harder estimate implied.** Winning DK lineup totals were 609.5 (2,216-entry Eagle) and 647.5 (79,274-entry mini-MAX), with six golfers clearing 98 FPTS and three clearing 110 (Michael Thorbjornsen 129.5, Matt Wallace 114.0, Davis Riley 111.0). That is a birdie-fest-shaped leaderboard, not a penal one. Treat "par 70 + narrower + 2–3 strokes harder" as an unproven vendor estimate that the first playing partly contradicted.
  - **The "no course history exists" read was correct and is the durable lesson.** The two biggest scores came from a 24-year-old with one prior Detroit start (Thorbjornsen) and a player no article named (Wallace). Old-course winner archetypes did not repeat: defending champion Aldrich Potgieter and 2023 winner Rickie Fowler were both rosterable and neither defined the slate (Fowler did score 91.0 and made the Eagle winner).
  - **The bomber narrative was a field story, not a scoring edge — confirming ETR's refusal to boost distance.** The field crowded the long hitters (Jake Knapp 20.1–26.9% owned, 45.0 FPTS; Chris Gotterup 26.7–29.7%, 27.5 FPTS) and both were top-3 fish traps. The scorers were iron players and accurate mid-range types, which matches the expected new identity (mid/long irons, avoid 3-putts) better than the distance read.
  - **Bigger greens compressed putting variance as predicted.** No cheap putter-driven lightning strike carried the top of the leaderboard; the winners' separation came from ball-striking scores in the 98–130 range rather than one 140+ outlier. The 2025-era "giant putting contest" description is now clearly dead.
  - **Winning structure ran LEANER on low-owned pieces than any logged Classic slate.** Eagle winners averaged 12.88% per-player ownership with only 1.55 sub-10%-owned golfers; mini-MAX top-100 averaged 12.13% with 1.67. Working read for the next unknown-course slate: when the venue itself voids all history, the whole board is already high-variance, so winners bought separation with roughly ONE well-chosen cheap piece, not two or three.
