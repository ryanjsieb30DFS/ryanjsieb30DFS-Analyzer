# TPC Southwind (Memphis, TN) — FedEx St. Jude Championship

Built from the 2026 St. Jude articles (ETR Course Fits & Preview 8/13/26, ETR Value Report 8/13/26, ETR Large Field Breakdown 8/13/26); **course profile verified against the 2026-08-19 autopsy** (accuracy archetype + long-driver docks held; see per-slate observations).

## Course profile
- Par 70, ~7,290 yards, Bermuda tee to green. Hosts the first FedEx Cup playoff event (top-70 field, no cut).
- Plays a shade OVER par for an adjusted field — modestly harder than the average Tour venue. Winning scores land ~-15 to -17 (first course since The Open where the winner likely finishes worse than -20).
- Difficulty is all tee-to-green: fairways ~29 yards wide vs ~34 Tour average (top-20 narrowest driving test), thick unpredictable Bermuda rough (top-20 in cost), water everywhere (16th on Tour in penalties), greens-in-regulation under 60% (bottom quartile).
- The greens are the release valve: putting ranks among the easiest 15% on Tour, 5–15 ft easier than average, forgiving bunkers, little undulation. Late-summer heat (~100°F) keeps conditions soft.

## Winning archetype
- **Accuracy off the tee + iron control (especially 150+ yards).** Distance grades neutral — a positional test, not a power one. ETR's fit adjustments favor short accurate drivers and lean against long inaccurate drivers.
- Ball-striking alone separates here; putting skill is NOT required to win (Zalatoris 2022 and peak-JT won here as poor putters — Value Report).
