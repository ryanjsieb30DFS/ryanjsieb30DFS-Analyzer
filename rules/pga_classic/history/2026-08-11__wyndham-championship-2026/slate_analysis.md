# Wyndham Championship — Sedgefield Country Club (PGA Classic)

## The short version

1. This is the last regular-season event, played on a short, tight, easy-scoring course where the winner usually reaches about 20 under par.
2. Rain has soaked the course, and soft conditions make the scoring even easier and shrink the edge of the accurate-driving specialists everyone is talking about.
3. Nearly half of all teams will start with the same star, Cameron Young, so the tournament inside the tournament is what you do around him.
4. The crowd is also piling onto a handful of cheap players who ran hot the last two weeks, and those hot-streak players are where the pricing is worst.
5. The one decision that most separates entries this week: take Young and get different in the five spots below him, or skip him and accept you are different at the top already.
6. A sharp (a proven winning player) mostly TAKES the popular star here, and buys his difference in the $7,000–$8,000 players almost nobody picks.
7. The biggest trap is Doug Ghim — a streaky player about one in five teams will pick because he was great the last two weeks, not because he projects to be great this week.
8. History says almost every winning golf lineup carries at least one player picked by fewer than 10% of teams, so a team made only of popular players cannot realistically win.
